# Code of Conduct

Evensies follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). It applies in issues, pull requests and every other project space, and to anyone representing the project elsewhere.

In short: be kind and patient, assume good intent, welcome people of every background and level of experience, and keep criticism about the work, not the person. Harassment, insults and personal attacks are not accepted.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour worries you, report it privately to the maintainer, [@devyourown](https://github.com/devyourown): open an issue that says only that you'd like to make a private report (no details), and the maintainer will get in touch to arrange a private channel.

Reports are kept confidential. For content that breaks GitHub's own rules you can also use GitHub's "Report content" option.

## Enforcement

The maintainer reviews every report and decides on a response following the Contributor Covenant's [enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines), from a private warning to a permanent ban from the project.
