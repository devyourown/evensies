# Privacy Policy (draft — review before store submission)

**Who:** Nari Information (Hyojun Lee), Republic of Korea. Contact: hyojoon56@gmail.com

**What we store:** your display name and the identifier your sign-in provider (Apple or Google) gives us; the groups, members, expenses and payments you and your group enter; a push-notification token for your device if you allow notifications; server logs (IP address, request path, timestamp) kept for 14 days for abuse prevention.

**What we do not do:** no advertising, no analytics SDKs, no selling or sharing of data with third parties. Data is sent to Apple/Google only for sign-in and to Firebase Cloud Messaging only to deliver push notifications.

**Where:** the hosted server runs in the EU (Hetzner, Germany). If you self-host, your data never reaches us.

**Your rights:** export everything from Settings → Export at any time; delete your account from Settings → Delete account (your name is removed; expenses you entered stay in the group as a placeholder so other members' balances remain correct). Contact us to exercise any further rights under GDPR or Korean PIPA.

**Children:** the app is not directed at children under 13.

**Changes:** we will update this page and the date below. Last updated: 2026-10-10.
